Data Exploration Ideas for Pump.fun Analytics Dashboard
========================================================

1. TOKEN-LEVEL EXPLORATION (not just creator-level)
----------------------------------------------------

a. Token Treemap / Token Table
   Right now the treemap is organized by creator. Add a toggle or separate view
   that flattens every individual token into its own rectangle, sized by market
   cap or ATH. This lets users spot high-performing tokens from smaller creators
   who'd be invisible in the creator-level view.

b. Token Search / Filter
   A search bar that finds any token by name, symbol, or mint address across all
   creators. Clicking a result navigates to the token detail panel or highlights
   the creator on the treemap.

c. "Rising Tokens" Feed
   Sort all tokens by ath_market_cap / usd_market_cap ratio (i.e. how close a
   token is to its ATH). Tokens near 1.0 are "at ATH" — a live signal.
   Alternatively, sort by last_trade_timestamp to show the most recently active
   tokens.


2. DERIVED METRICS & RANKINGS
------------------------------

d. Bond Rate Leaderboard
   bonded / token_count — the percentage of a creator's tokens that successfully
   bonded. A creator who bonds 8/10 tokens is far more interesting than one who
   bonds 8/500. This is a strong "quality" signal.

e. ATH Efficiency
   total_ath_market_cap / token_count — average peak market cap per token.
   Identifies creators who consistently produce high-cap tokens, not just volume.

f. "Hit Rate" Score
   Composite score combining bond rate, average ATH, and follower count. Weight
   them however you like. This becomes a single "alpha" number for ranking.

g. Recency-Weighted Activity
   Weight tokens by how recently they were created (created_timestamp). A creator
   who bonded 5 tokens last week is more relevant than one who bonded 5 tokens
   six months ago.

h. "Dead Token" Ratio
   Tokens where last_trade_timestamp is very old (e.g. >7 days ago) and not
   bonded. dead_tokens / token_count shows how many of a creator's launches
   fizzled out.


3. ALTERNATE VISUALIZATIONS
-----------------------------

i. Scatter Plot: Bond Rate vs. ATH per Token
   X-axis = bond rate, Y-axis = avg ATH market cap, bubble size = token count.
   Each bubble is a creator. This instantly separates quality creators (top-right)
   from spam deployers (bottom-left).

j. Timeline / Activity Heatmap
   Plot token created_timestamp on a calendar heatmap (like GitHub contributions).
   Shows when a creator is most active, and reveals patterns like "deploys 20
   tokens every Monday."

k. Network Graph: Creator Social Connections
   You already fetch followers and following for each creator. Build a
   force-directed graph where nodes are creators and edges are follow
   relationships. Clusters reveal "deployer communities" or coordinated groups.

l. Stacked Area Chart: Creator Market Cap Over Time
   Using created_timestamp and ath_market_cap_timestamp from each token, build a
   time series of cumulative market cap growth per creator. Shows who's on an
   uptrend vs. peaked and faded.


4. FILTERING & SEGMENTATION
-----------------------------

m. Multi-Filter Sidebar
   Add combinable filters:
   - Min/max token count
   - Min/max bonded count
   - Min/max bond rate (%)
   - Has X/Twitter account (yes/no)
   - Has PFP (already done)
   - Min followers
   - Created tokens in last N days
   - Min ATH market cap

   This lets users build their own queries like "creators with >50% bond rate,
   >1K followers, active in last 7 days."

n. "Tiers" or Segments
   Automatically bucket creators into tiers: Whale (>$1M total ATH),
   Dolphin ($100K-$1M), Fish ($10K-$100K), Plankton (<$10K). Show tier
   distribution as a pie chart or let users toggle which tiers are visible on
   the treemap.

o. "New Deployers" Tab
   Filter to creators whose first token created_timestamp is within the last
   7/30 days. Fresh deployers who are already bonding tokens are high-signal
   alpha.


5. COMPARISON & BENCHMARKING
------------------------------

p. Side-by-Side Creator Comparison
   Select 2-3 creators and see their metrics in parallel columns: token count,
   bond rate, ATH, followers, etc. Like a "vs." mode.

q. Creator Percentile Ranks
   For each metric, show where a creator sits relative to the whole dataset.
   "Top 3% by bond rate, top 12% by ATH." This contextualizes raw numbers.


6. SOCIAL & NARRATIVE SIGNALS
-------------------------------

r. "Has Twitter" vs. "No Twitter" Toggle
   Similar to "Has PFP" — creators with an X account attached are making a
   public identity bet, which correlates with seriousness.

s. Bio / Description Word Cloud
   Aggregate all token description fields for a creator and render a word cloud.
   Reveals themes (e.g. "AI", "meme", "dog") and helps categorize deployers by
   niche.

t. Reply Count Heatmap
   The PumpFunCoin data includes reply_count. Tokens with high reply counts have
   community engagement. Surface creators whose tokens consistently generate
   discussion.


7. ALERTS & WATCHLISTS
------------------------

u. Watchlist
   Let users star/bookmark specific creators and see a dedicated view of just
   their watchlist. Persisted in localStorage.

v. "Just Bonded" Feed
   Poll periodically and highlight any token where complete just flipped to true.
   Real-time alpha — a token just hit Raydium.

w. Threshold Alerts
   "Notify me when any creator with >70% bond rate launches a new token." Could
   be client-side polling with browser notifications.


8. DATA EXPORT & DEEP DIVES
-----------------------------

x. CSV/JSON Export
   One-click export of the full dataset (or current filtered view) to CSV or
   JSON. Power users will want to pull this into their own spreadsheets or
   scripts.

y. "Deep Dive" Mode
   Click any creator to fetch their full DeployerAnalytics (you already have the
   /api/deployer/[creator] route) and show: portfolio PnL, SOL balance, every
   token they hold, every token they created, and their full follower list. This
   is partially implemented in the /data page's ExpandedDetail but could be
   surfaced more prominently.


9. TOKEN-SPECIFIC SIGNALS FROM EXISTING FIELDS
-------------------------------------------------

z. Volatility Score Distribution
   PumpFunCoin has volatility_score. Show a histogram of volatility across all
   tokens, or let users filter the treemap to only show creators whose tokens
   have high/low volatility.

aa. NSFW / Banned Filter
    nsfw and is_banned fields exist. Toggle to hide or isolate these. Some users
    may want to filter out banned tokens; others might find the "banned but high
    market cap" segment interesting.

ab. Real SOL Reserves
    real_sol_reserves shows actual liquidity. Creators whose tokens have high
    real reserves relative to market cap are more "real." Could compute a
    liquidity ratio.


10. THE "GALAXY" VIEW
-----------------------

ac. Full Universe Visualization
    Instead of a treemap (which inherently emphasizes the biggest), render every
    single creator as a dot in a 2D scatter space (e.g. t-SNE or UMAP reduced
    from their multi-dimensional metrics). Clusters would naturally form around
    similar creator profiles. This is the most ambitious but would be a genuine
    differentiator — no one else is doing this with pump.fun data.


PRIORITY RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------
The lowest-hanging fruit with the most immediate impact:
  - d (bond rate)
  - m (multi-filter sidebar)
  - o (new deployers)
  - a (token-level treemap)

These all use data already available and don't require any new API endpoints.
